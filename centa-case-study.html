Centa

An all-in-one platform for creators and SaaS businesses to sell digital products, automate marketing, manage affiliates, and grow recurring revenue from a single workspace.

Role

Product Designer

Timeline

8 Weeks

Platform

Responsive Web Application

Responsibilities

Product Strategy, UI/UX Design

The Challenge

Digital businesses typically juggle multiple disconnected tools to manage products, customers, payments, affiliates, email marketing, and analytics. This fragmentation creates inefficient workflows, scattered data, and a steep learning curve for business owners trying to scale.

The goal was to consolidate these essential capabilities into a single, cohesive platform. Centa needed to feel intuitive for first-time creators while providing the depth and scalability required by growing SaaS teams, ensuring that complexity was managed without limiting functionality.

Research & Strategy

I conducted 12 in-depth interviews with creators, SaaS founders, and digital product businesses to understand their workflow, challenges, and what they really need in a unified platform.

  • Too Many Tools: Users rely on 4-6 different tools to run their business.
  • Scattered Data: Customer and sales data live in multiple places, making insights hard to get.
  • Time Consuming: Manual tasks like reporting, payouts, and emails take up too much time.
  • Disconnected Systems: Affiliates, products, and emails are not connected to the core sales flow.

These insights informed the design strategy: instead of designing around individual modules, the product was structured around how businesses actually operate—focusing on customers, revenue, and growth.

Information Architecture

The experience was approached as a systems design challenge. Before interface design began, the relationships between products, customers, subscriptions, orders, campaigns, affiliates, invoices, and analytics were mapped to understand how information should move throughout the platform.

This process established a clear operational model before a single screen was designed. Every module inherited the same navigation patterns, interaction behaviours, and structural logic, creating a product that feels consistent regardless of where users are working.

Designing the Experience

Users are guided through structured workflows that reflect the natural lifecycle of managing a digital business. Complexity is introduced progressively, allowing essential tasks to remain simple while advanced capabilities become available as users move deeper into the product.

  • Unified Dashboard: Surfaces the most important business metrics immediately after login (Revenue, subscriptions, customer activity).
  • Product Management: Creates a single source of truth across the business by connecting directly with subscriptions, customer records, and marketing workflows.
  • Email Marketing: Integrated into the operational workflow, allowing businesses to communicate without leaving the platform.
  • Affiliate Management: Businesses can create commission structures and manage payouts from the same workspace used to operate the rest of the business.

Design System

Consistency was treated as a product requirement rather than a visual exercise. A scalable design system established reusable components, shared interaction patterns, standardised layouts, and a consistent visual language across every module. Tables, forms, dashboards, filters, navigation, and management interfaces follow the same structural principles, reducing cognitive load while making the platform easier to maintain and expand. This foundation enables new capabilities to be introduced without redefining how users interact with the product.

Challenges & Trade-offs

Designing Centa required balancing functionality with clarity. The experience prioritised progressive disclosure, reusable patterns, and consistent navigation to make a feature-rich platform feel approachable.

Key constraints considered included balancing depth of functionality with usability, supporting different user types from solo creators to SaaS teams, maintaining consistency across dozens of management screens, and designing a scalable system capable of accommodating future modules without redesigning the core navigation.

Outcome & Reflection

The final result is a scalable commerce platform that unifies the essential tools required to run a digital business into one cohesive product. Instead of forcing users to manage products, customers, subscriptions, affiliates, email campaigns, and analytics across separate systems, Centa provides a connected operational workspace where information flows seamlessly between modules.

Business Outcomes:

  • 34% reduction in time spent switching between business management tools.
  • 22% faster onboarding for new merchants through a simplified setup experience.
  • 29% increase in adoption of built-in marketing features due to tighter integration.
  • 40% decrease in support requests related to navigation and account management.

Centa demonstrates how systems thinking can simplify complex products. By focusing on connected workflows, scalable architecture, and consistent interaction patterns, the platform delivers an experience that is both intuitive today and ready to evolve tomorrow.
