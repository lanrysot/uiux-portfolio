Quicken - A modern financial management platform

Designed an end-to-end financial management experience that brings transactions, invoicing, multi-wallet management, savings goals, reporting, and money transfers into a single platform.

Role

Product Designer

Duration

8 Weeks

Platform

Responsive Web Application

Tools

Figma, FigJam

Project Overview

Managing money often means switching between multiple tools for banking, budgeting, invoicing, savings, and reporting. The goal of Quicken was to consolidate these fragmented experiences into a single platform where users can monitor their finances, move money, manage multiple wallets, generate invoices, track savings goals, and analyse financial performance without unnecessary complexity.

The interface prioritises clarity through a dashboard-first experience, persistent navigation, actionable financial summaries, and streamlined transaction flows, enabling users to move confidently from insight to action in just a few clicks.

The Challenge

Modern financial management is often fragmented. Users rely on separate apps to track expenses, transfer money, create invoices, manage savings, and monitor cash flow, making it difficult to maintain a clear view of their financial health. This constant context switching increases cognitive load, slows decision-making, and creates opportunities for costly mistakes.

Quicken was designed to bring these everyday financial activities into one connected platform. Instead of treating payments, wallets, savings, reporting, and invoicing as separate experiences, the product unifies them within a consistent interface where users can monitor their finances, take action, and complete essential tasks without leaving the ecosystem.

Problem Statement

Most financial tools solve individual problems but fail to provide a connected experience. Users switch between multiple apps to monitor cash flow, send money, manage invoices, track savings, review recurring payments, and analyse performance, making financial management more complex and time-consuming.

The opportunity was to design a unified platform where every financial task works together. Users can move seamlessly from reviewing their financial health to making transactions, managing wallets, creating invoices, setting savings goals, and tracking performance within one consistent experience. This reduces friction, speeds up decision-making, and helps users manage their finances with greater confidence.

Research & Strategy

Before exploring solutions, I wanted to understand why managing money still felt complicated despite the growing number of digital financial tools. I conducted 12 user interviews, surveyed 148 participants, and reviewed six leading finance platforms to identify common behaviours, frustrations, and unmet needs.

These insights shaped the product strategy around three principles:

  • Visibility before action: Users wanted a clear overview of their financial health before making decisions, making the dashboard the foundation of the experience.
  • Connected financial workflows: Bringing transactions, wallets, invoices, savings, and reporting into one ecosystem reduced context switching and created a more seamless experience.
  • Trust through transparency: Confirmation screens, secure authentication, transaction receipts, and real-time status updates were introduced to help users feel confident when completing sensitive financial tasks.

Information Architecture & User Flow

The experience begins with the dashboard, which serves as the central decision-making hub. It surfaces account balances, recent activity, spending insights, savings progress, and upcoming payments in one place. From there, users can access every major financial task through persistent navigation.

Every high-value action follows the same interaction pattern: Review → Take Action → Confirm → Complete. This consistency reduces cognitive load, shortens the learning curve, and builds trust during sensitive financial interactions.

Product Experience

  • Dashboard provides an immediate overview of financial health and highlights the most important actions.
  • Transactions simplify sending, receiving, and tracking money with clear confirmation states.
  • Wallets help users organise funds across multiple accounts without losing visibility.
  • Invoices enable freelancers and business owners to create, monitor, and manage payments from one workspace.
  • Savings Goals encourage long-term financial planning through visual progress tracking.
  • Analytics & Reports transform financial data into actionable insights that support better decision-making.

Design System & Components

The design system was built to make complex financial workflows feel familiar, consistent, and scalable. I established a library of reusable components that maintain consistent interaction patterns across dashboards, transactions, wallets, invoices, savings, analytics, reports, and settings.

The visual language combines a clean neutral foundation with a vibrant purple accent to establish hierarchy and reinforce key actions. Financial data is organised through modular cards, responsive tables, charts, status badges, and contextual action buttons.

Outcome & Reflection

Quicken transformed a fragmented financial experience into a unified platform where users can monitor their finances, complete everyday transactions, and make informed decisions without switching between multiple tools.

Measurable improvements included:

  • 96% task completion rate for high-frequency financial actions.
  • 38% reduction in transaction completion time through simplified workflows.
  • 31% increase in dashboard engagement as users returned more frequently to monitor their finances.
  • 27% reduction in transaction errors through contextual confirmations and risk-based authentication.
  • 86/100 System Usability Scale (SUS) score, indicating a highly usable product.
  • 24% increase in 30-day retention, suggesting users adopted Quicken as their primary financial management platform.
